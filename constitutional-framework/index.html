Constitutional Foundations

Explore the historical evolution, constitutional philosophy and foundational principles of the Constitution of India.

This module provides the conceptual and legal foundation for understanding India's constitutional system. It connects constitutional history, institutional design, rights, duties and the limits of constitutional power.

How to Study This Module

  • Understand the historical circumstances and constitutional ideas behind the making of the Constitution.
  • Read constitutional provisions together with their underlying principles and institutional purposes.
  • Connect Fundamental Rights, Directive Principles and Fundamental Duties as parts of a common constitutional framework.
  • Study constitutional amendments and important judicial interpretations to understand how constitutional principles evolve.
  • Distinguish the text of the Constitution from judicial doctrines developed through constitutional interpretation.

UPSC Examination Relevance

This module supports both factual understanding of constitutional provisions for Prelims and conceptual analysis of constitutional values, doctrines, amendments and judicial interpretation for GS-II Mains.
